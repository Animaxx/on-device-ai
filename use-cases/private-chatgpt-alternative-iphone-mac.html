Privacy-First AI

Private ChatGPT Alternative for iPhone and Mac

Use local models for private chat, documents, notes, transcripts, and saved research on Apple devices. Add cloud providers only when you choose to.

Core Difference: Cloud-first chatbots process your prompts on remote servers. On Device AI lets you run local workflows on your Apple device, so private files, notes, and chats can stay on your hardware.

1. No Account Required for Local Workflows

Download the app, choose a local model, and start a private conversation. Local chat history and project files stay in app-managed storage on your device.

2. How it Compares: Cloud vs. Local-First

Evaluate the benefits of switching to a local-first architecture for your daily workflows:

Feature Traditional Cloud AI On Device AI (Local)
Data Storage Remote servers (subject to training) Local app storage
Internet Required Yes (constantly online) No (runs fully offline)
Telemetry & Logs Exhaustive session tracking Zero analytics, zero tracking
Core Cost Usually subscription-based Free download, optional upgrades

3. Optional Cloud Providers

When a task needs a remote model, configure your own cloud provider. You decide which conversations use cloud models, and sensitive projects can stay on local models.

4. Notes, Search, and Automation

Use Knowledge Libraries for private documents, Text Notes for quick capture, AI Search for source-backed research, and Shortcuts for common speech or note workflows.
